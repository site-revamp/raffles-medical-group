More patient FAQs ↗
How do I make an appointment?

Use the service-specific appointment routes above. For specialist appointments, call +65 6311 1222. Other services have their own booking and enquiry options on the Raffles contact page .

Where can I find clinic locations?

Use the Raffles clinic finder for locations, opening hours and clinic contact details. Check the individual clinic's information before visiting.

How can I explore health screening?

Visit Raffles Health Screening to review services and packages. Contact the screening team through the published appointment options if you need help.

Is support available for overseas patients?

Raffles has international patient representative offices . Visit the directory to find the relevant office and its contact details.
