# Raffles Medical Group — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Raffles Medical Group is a Singapore-founded integrated private healthcare group providing primary, specialist and hospital care, alongside dental services, health screening, insurance and corporate healthcare.

The website serves patients, families, preventive-health customers, international patients and employers. The redesigned homepage makes care options easier to explore and clarifies the routes to relevant services and appointments.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Earlier Access to Care Choices

Patient care choices now appear directly below the hero, with news further down the page. Previously, four “Latest News” items and the “Why Choose Us” section preceded the main service overview. The revised order helps visitors find relevant care sooner.

### 2. Clearer Appointment Routes

Service-specific appointment choices provide clear destinations and relevant telephone alternatives. This addresses the previous generic “Book an Appointment” action, which opened a JavaScript modal while different service routes were explained elsewhere. Visitors can identify the appropriate appointment channel at the point of action.

### 3. Fewer Competing Notices and Promotions

Important advisory information is presented compactly, while promotional content sits within the page flow. This addresses the simultaneous doctor-impersonation notice and LASIK promotional overlay observed on the previous homepage, reducing interruptions during patient tasks.

## Design Improvements

- **Layout and hierarchy:** Care choices follow the hero, while news appears lower on the page.
- **Navigation consistency:** Descriptive appointment choices clearly distinguish the available service routes.
- **Visual presentation:** Compact advisories and promotions within the page flow reduce competing content layers.
- **User experience:** The revised content order supports a clearer progression from exploring care to arranging an appointment.
